    Help & about

    ✕
    1

    Open a creator's Posts page on patreon.com. A panel appears bottom-right.

    2

    Click Scan to collect that creator's posts and metadata.

    3

    Open this dashboard, pick files, and hit Download.

    Files are organized into folders per creator and post (including thumbnail, video, comments, description, and attached files).

    A one-time local helper lets the extension save to any folder and download external videos (YouTube/Vimeo). Without it, downloads can't run reliably. Set it up under Settings → Bridge.

    Automatic file extraction and direct downloads are integrated for the following cloud services:

    Rate Limits & Provider Restrictions: Public file hosters (especially PixelDrain, MediaFire, MEGA & Google Drive) may temporarily restrict high-volume downloads, enforce Captchas, or trigger rate-limit errors (403/429). If an automated download fails, please wait a short period or open the direct link saved in _download_links.txt.
